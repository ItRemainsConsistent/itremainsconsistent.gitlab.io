## About this webpage

Right now I use this page as a repository of PDFs for the talks and posters I present at conferences.

### Why does this even have an RSS feed?

This webpage started as a math blog, but there was never a proper math post written for it...

I set it up very few months into my PhD studies, as an excuse to have outside pressure to write things up in order to understand them more deeply.
I wanted to cover the topics I was currently researching, but a number of factors stopped me from doing that (factors such as lack of time or motivation due to life issues, potential unauthorized use of my unpublished research as AI training, etc).
This might change again in the future, though!

Thankfully, I was able to find sources of that "outside pressure" I mentioned earlier in places I feel more comfortable with --- such as preparing talks and posters for conferences or organizing reading seminars.

### Implementation

The internals use [Hakyll](https://jaspervdj.be/hakyll/) as a static site generator and [Giscus](https://giscus.app/) as a comment system.
As mentioned in the [Initial Commit](/initial-commit), the codebase was forked (with permission) from [Isaac Elliott's blog](https://github.com/LightAndLight/lightandlight.github.io).

This webpage is deployed on GitLab Pages, and the source code is available on both [GitLab](https://gitlab.com/ItRemainsConsistent/itremainsconsistent.gitlab.io) and [GitHub](https://github.com/ItRemainsConsistent/itremainsconsistent.gitlab.io).
